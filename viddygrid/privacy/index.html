ViddyGrid — Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 August 2026

ViddyGrid collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. Nothing about you, your files or your use of the app is sent anywhere, because the app contains no code that could send it.

What is collected

Nothing. That is the whole answer, and the rest of this page exists to say precisely what it means rather than to qualify it.

What the app can reach on your Mac

ViddyGrid runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It can read the folder of videos you choose and the individual files you open, and nothing else. Its access to that folder is read-only — it is not technically capable of writing to, renaming or deleting your video files, and this is enforced by macOS rather than promised by the app.

What is stored, and where

Everything ViddyGrid produces stays inside its own container on your Mac, under ~/Library/Containers/. That is:

  • A local index of your library — file paths, titles, durations and transcripts — in a SQLite database only this app opens.
  • A bookmark recording which folder you chose, so the app can reopen it next time.
  • Interface preferences: window size, column widths, subtitle and playback settings.

None of it is transmitted, backed up to any service of mine, or readable by any other application. Deleting the app removes it. There is no server-side copy, because there is no server.

Transcription happens on your Mac

ViddyGrid transcribes speech using Apple's on-device speech recognition, built into macOS. Your audio is never uploaded, never sent to a transcription service, and never processed by anyone else's computer. There is no API key involved, no cloud account, and no third-party AI service — not mine and not anybody's.

The one time the network is used

The first time you transcribe something, macOS may need to install its English speech model if your Mac does not already have it. ViddyGrid asks the system to do that, and macOS downloads the model from Apple.

This is a download, not an upload. Nothing about you, your files or your library is included in it, and it is a request for a standard macOS component rather than anything specific to you. It happens once. If your Mac already has the model, or has no internet connection, no request is made at all.

Apart from that, the app makes no network connections of any kind. It contains no networking code — no URLSession, no sockets — and the media decoder it ships with is built with network protocol support disabled entirely, so it can read files from your disk and nothing else.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no attribution framework, no embedded browser, no social login. The open-source libraries ViddyGrid includes — FFmpeg, dav1d, zimg, libzvbi and GRDB — decode video and store data locally; none of them is a service and none of them phones anyone. Their licence texts are shown inside the app, under ViddyGrid › Licences.

Children

ViddyGrid is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. I hold none, so there is nothing to access or erase — and no request you could make that I would be able to answer with anything other than this page. Your library index is on your own disk and under your own control.

Changes to this policy

If ViddyGrid ever does something that changes this page, this page changes first and the date at the top changes with it. A version that collected anything would be a different product.
